## Processing by GitHub
When the website is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary data (e.g. IP address, browser information, timestamp) as an independent controller, in order to to provide the website, ensure its security, and protect against misuse.
The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

In this context, personal data is regularly transferred to the United States.
GitHub relies on the [EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/) for such transfers.

Further details on the categories of data processed, retention periods, recipients and international data transfers can be found in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
